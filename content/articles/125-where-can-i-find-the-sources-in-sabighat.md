Ahmad Yusuf al-Sayyid developed *Sabighat* from courses on responding to questions about faith. The edition used here is the third, published by Takween Studies and Research in 2017 (1438 AH), ISBN 978-603-01-9940-2. [Book page on the author’s website](https://ahmadalsayed.net/books/7?tab=1)

When reading an answer, start with the printed page cited for Al-Sayyid’s argument. A Quran or hadith reference takes you to the text the answer discusses. A commentary or scholarly work helps you see how a reader has understood that text. The bibliography below follows the printed pages. Repeated records for the same edition appear once.
