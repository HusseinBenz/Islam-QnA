Quran 5:38 names amputation as a penalty for theft, and the following verse speaks of repentance and reform. The wording raises a question the classical commentators discussed in detail. Which acts count as theft for the purpose of this particular penalty?

Al-Tabari and al-Qurtubi examine Quranic wording alongside reports about which cases qualified. They record disagreement about details such as the minimum value of the property. Classical jurists treated this as a matter of adjudication. Their discussions of the rule and its limits help explain how it was understood.

*Sabighat* identifies the severity of the penalty as an objection in its overview. The primary sources below give the context for that question.
