There are two questions here. **Why create?** Al-Sayyid argues that the order and variety of life make purposeful creation a more fitting interpretation than a purposeless act. In that interpretation, God gives people ways to know him, through reflection on the world and human experience, and through revelation.

**Why ask for worship?** In the Qur’an, God is self-sufficient, while creatures depend on him. Worship is how a person acknowledges that relationship through gratitude, humility and action. It includes prayer and other acts done for God. Qur’an 39:7 brings the two ideas together by describing God as independent of people while still caring how they respond.

The book, drawing on Abdullah al-Shahri, connects that relationship with accountability. If God is the source of life and guidance, how a person freely responds matters. Qur’an 21:16–23 frames creation as purposeful and places human beings within a world answerable to God.
