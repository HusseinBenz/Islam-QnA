Try a simple test with a claim you see online. Write down exactly what it says. Find the earliest source you can reach. Examine how the source knows it and whether another independent source confirms it. Then ask whether the evidence supports the whole claim or only part of it.

Islamic scholarship gives one example. Hadith scholars examined the people in chains of transmission, compared versions, looked for gaps, and tested seemingly reliable reports for mistakes. Manuals on the ethics and methods of debate likewise asked what counts as a defensible argument.

Practise on ordinary claims from school, friends, or a news feed. Use a library as well as a search engine, and learn how to locate an original hadith before relying on someone’s account of it. Greater skill means having better reasons each time you accept, question, or set aside a claim.
