Think of a sunset seen from shore. The sun appears to sink into the water. Ordinary speech can describe that sight directly. In Qur’an 18:86, the traveler Dhū al-Qarnayn reaches a western destination and “finds” the sun setting in a muddy spring. The verb puts the description in his field of view.

Ibn Kathir compares it to a sunset seen from a coast. Al-Qurtubi records an explanation that the sun is larger than Earth and that the expression describes what appears to the eye. Al-Baydawi and al-Jalalayn give similar readings. Some of these commentators also accepted premodern astronomy. Their observation about the wording still shows that this reading was available centuries before modern astronomy.
