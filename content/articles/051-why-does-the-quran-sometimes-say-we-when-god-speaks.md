A word can have more than one use. In Arabic, a speaker can use a plural form to express majesty. Quran 15:9 uses it when God speaks about revealing and preserving the Quran. Where the question is how many gods there are, other verses speak directly. Quran 112:1 declares God one, and Quran 4:171 repeats that there is one God.

The Quran describes this approach in 3:7. Some verses are clear guides and other verses can admit more than one reading at first glance. As Ibn Kathir explains, the clear passages help readers weigh the possible meanings of the less clear ones. The same method helps whenever a claim rests on a verse in isolation.
