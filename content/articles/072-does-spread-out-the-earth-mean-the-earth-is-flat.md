Look across a field and the ground may appear flat for a long distance. Viewed at the scale of the whole planet, that ground is a small part of a curved surface. Both descriptions can be true at their own scale.

Al-Razi makes this point in his commentary on Qur’an 13:3. He says a very large sphere can look like a flat surface to someone standing on one small part. Ibn Hazm also reports that the Muslim scholars he regarded as authorities accepted a spherical Earth. The familiar expression about land spreading before a person can be read in light of ordinary experience and the physical evidence for Earth’s shape.
