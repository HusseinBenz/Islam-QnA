It is a fair question. If God is perfect and complete, why would He command people to praise Him and bow to Him? Is that not a kind of vanity?

## God gains nothing from our worship

The Quran answers directly:

> *"O mankind, it is you who are in need of Allah, while Allah is the Free of need, the Praiseworthy."* — Quran 35:15

In a sacred hadith, God says: "O My servants, if the first and the last of you, the humans and the jinn among you, were all as pious as the most pious heart of any one of you, that would not add anything to My dominion. And if they were all as wicked as the most wicked heart, that would not take anything from it" (Sahih Muslim 2577).

So the question changes. It is not "why does God need worship?" but "why do *we* need it?"

> *"Whoever strives, strives only for his own soul. Allah is free of need of all the worlds."* — Quran 29:6

## Everyone worships something

A human being cannot live without a centre: something loved most, feared most and obeyed first. If that centre is not God, it will be something else, such as money, approval, a career, a relationship, or one's own desires. The Quran asks:

> *"Have you seen the one who takes his own desire as his god?"* — Quran 45:23

These masters never say "enough". They demand more, and they give no rest. The Quran compares a person who serves many masters to a servant owned by quarrelling partners, and a person who serves One to a servant who belongs wholly to a single master, and asks: are the two equal? (39:29)

This is why worship is freedom. When the Muslim envoy Rib'i ibn 'Amir stood before the Persian general Rustam and was asked why his people had come, he replied that God had sent them to bring people out of servitude to other people and into the service of the Lord of all people, and from the narrowness of this world to its wideness.

## What worship does for the worshipper

Each act of worship is described in the Quran by what it gives *us*:

- **Prayer** keeps a person from shameful and wrongful deeds (29:45) and is a daily appointment with the One who made you (20:14).
- **Fasting** is prescribed "that you may become mindful of God" (2:183).
- **Charity** purifies the giver and makes the heart grow (9:103).
- **Remembrance** brings the calm that nothing else brings (13:28).

## Gratitude is simply the truth

If someone gave you your eyes, you would thank them for the rest of your life. God gave you your eyes, your mind, your mother's love, and every breath since. Worship is what honesty looks like once you realise how much you have been given.

And God meets the smallest step with generosity. He says in a sacred hadith: "If My servant comes to Me walking, I go to him running" (Sahih al-Bukhari 7405).
