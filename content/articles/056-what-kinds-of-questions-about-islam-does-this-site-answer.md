Some questions are about *what* Islam teaches. Others are about *how we know* what it teaches. Many arguments get stuck because the two are mixed up. Someone troubled by a legal penalty, for example, may really be asking whether the law is moral, or whether its source is authentic, or how scholars interpreted it. Each needs a different kind of answer. This map groups the questions so you can find the one you are actually asking.

## Overlaps

Some subjects belong to more than one group. A question about apostasy, for example, can be a question about morality (Foundations) or about how the ruling was derived (Sources and interpretation). If one answer does not seem to address your concern, try the other group.
