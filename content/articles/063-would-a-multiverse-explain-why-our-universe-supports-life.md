Imagine many universes with different conditions. Observers would arise only in those where life was possible. This is the **selection effect** behind one response to fine-tuning, the question of why physical constants fall in ranges compatible with life.

A useful follow-up is what the model predicts and how those predictions can be tested. It also matters how a theory produces the universes and how it assigns probabilities to their properties. The book illustrates these open questions with a discussion between Richard Dawkins and physicist Steven Weinberg about the constants and the large number of possible settings. Their conversation illustrates the debate at that time.

If a multiverse model gained strong support, it would help explain some physical features of our universe. Whether the multiverse itself and the physical principles behind it have a further explanation would still be an open philosophical question.
