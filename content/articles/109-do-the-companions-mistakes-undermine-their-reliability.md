The Battle of the Camel and the Battle of Siffin were real conflicts in early Muslim history. The details of any particular allegation still depend on the evidence for it.

The book begins with the Sunni understanding of integrity. A Companion could make a serious mistake. A claim about what someone did during a conflict, or what Muhammad said about that person, needs a reliable source before it can support a judgment. Al-Sayyid asks readers to use the same standard for an account they find persuasive and one they would prefer to reject.

The book also points to the role of repentance. In one report about a woman from Juhayna, Muhammad praises the depth of her repentance after a serious wrong. For the author, the response to a wrong tells us something about a person alongside the act itself. The book sees this as compatible with the Sunni presumption of integrity in reporting.
