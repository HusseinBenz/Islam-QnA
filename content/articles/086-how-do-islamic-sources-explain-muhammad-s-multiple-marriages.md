The general rule in Qur’an 4:3 makes fair treatment a condition of plural marriage and directs a man to marry one if he fears injustice. Qur’an 33:50–52 then speaks to Muhammad’s own circumstances, naming categories of marriage and placing a limit on later marriages. Islamic legal tradition treats those verses as instructions specific to his household.

The reasons given in the sources differ by case. Qur’an 33:37 connects the marriage to Zaynab with a change in rules concerning a former wife of an adopted son. In a report in Muslim, Umm Salama recalls marrying Muhammad after she was widowed. These sources give readers particular cases to examine. A single explanation for every marriage would go beyond what these accounts can establish.
