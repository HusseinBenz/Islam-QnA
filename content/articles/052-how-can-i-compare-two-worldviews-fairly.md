question: How can I compare two worldviews fairly?
summary: Ask both views the same questions and look at what their answers can explain. A difficulty in one view is a reason to investigate the alternatives using the same standard.

Try three questions. **What happens to a wrong that human courts never address?** Islam includes judgment after death and restoration of rights. A view without an afterlife can still condemn the harm and seek accountability during life. **Why is the world intelligible?** Scientific research explains many patterns, from inheritance to the regularities physicists model. The further questions of why those patterns exist and whether they point to purpose are philosophical. **What kind of guidance did Muhammad leave?** Readers who rely on the Quran alone can ask how their view understands its commands to obey the Messenger and how it explains practices historically transmitted through the Sunnah.

Give each answer its strongest fair reading. Then write down what remains open and what evidence could change your mind. The author uses this comparison to explain why leaving a position does not, by itself, resolve the issue that prompted the change.
