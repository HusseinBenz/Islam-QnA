**Check the report.** Al-Sayyid examines four allegations used to discredit transmitters or the Sunnah. He finds insufficient evidence for reports that Muhammad cursed Mu‘awiyah, that ‘Umar accused Abu Hurayrah of theft, and that ‘Umar burned records of hadith. He also questions the story told about Abu Hurayrah in connection with the saying that visiting less often increases affection. A collection can record a report so that readers can assess it.

**Check what follows.** Quran 6:38 says nothing was neglected in “the Book.” Al-Sayyid reads this as God’s record of creation, drawing on the context. He discusses the reference to the revealed Book in Quran 16:89 separately. A hadith that discourages writing material other than the Quran expressly permits oral transmission. The existence of such a report does not settle whether prophetic teaching has authority. If someone cites that hadith while denying hadith authority, distinguish an argument for their own position from an argument using premises their audience already accepts.
