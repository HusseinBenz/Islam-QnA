Two readers can ask about the same hadith and have different questions. One might ask whether the words can be traced to Muhammad. Another might ask what they mean or how they apply. The book separates the authority of the Sunnah, the reliability of reports and the interpretation of those reports.

That distinction matters beyond hadith. A dispute over an account of a miracle or the life after death may turn on the report's reliability. A dispute over a legal rule may turn on the meaning of a text or the strength of a claim that scholars agreed about it.

Al-Sayyid uses “established” within a Sunni framework. Where the evidence or the legal interpretation is disputed, identifying the disagreement is part of explaining the issue.
