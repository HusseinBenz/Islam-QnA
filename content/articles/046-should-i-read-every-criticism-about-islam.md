The book distinguishes between preparing a specialist to study a controversy and helping a general reader understand a common objection. A specialist may need the original books and talks, the history of a claim, and how different writers defend it. If you are getting oriented, Al-Sayyid suggests choosing material using three checks. Is the question common or relevant? Does the author present it briefly and then develop the response? Do qualified people consider the response sound?

Al-Sayyid recalls al-Dhahabi’s observation that a challenging idea can catch a person's attention before they have tools to assess it. If you choose to read a substantial criticism, notice what you are learning and how much time goes to re-reading the same claims. Write down the unresolved point, then look for the material best suited to it.
