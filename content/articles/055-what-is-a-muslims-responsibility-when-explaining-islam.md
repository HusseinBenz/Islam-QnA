The Quran responds directly to questions, such as why the revelation came in stages. Quran 25:32 says the gradual revelation strengthened Muhammad’s heart. The Quran also states its disagreement with the view that God has a child in 19:88–95. Al-Sayyid sees answering objections clearly as part of the work of prophets and teachers. He uses Quran 25:52 to describe an intellectual effort carried out through scripture. Start with the main point and say when a particular explanation represents one scholar’s judgment.

The quality of the exchange matters. Quran 3:159 links harshness with people turning away. You can be accountable for your effort, accuracy, and conduct. The other person decides how to respond. For someone mainly trying to understand Islam, a respectful conversation can itself be informative.
